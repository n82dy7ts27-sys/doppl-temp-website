# Third-party notices

## Optical rendering

The fluting and grain approach in `components/optical-field.tsx` is adapted from **Fluted Glass**, authored by Paper Design / 21st Shader Builder. The source identifies its Paper Shaders basis as Apache-2.0.

- Component: https://21st.dev/@paper-design/components/fluted-glass
- Paper Shaders: https://shaders.paper.design/fluted-glass
- Licence: `public/licenses/apache-2.0.txt`

DOPPL adaptations include shape masks, palettes, sliced oval and overlapping rectangle compositions, controlled motion, pointer interaction, reduced-motion handling, resource cleanup and static fallbacks. The Dave Hoskins grain hash formulation is retained from the source.
